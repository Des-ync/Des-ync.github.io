03 / How I workThree things that
hold every time

01

Start where the money already moves

Nobody is going to change how they get paid for your product. Every build starts from MoMo, WhatsApp and cash — the rails people are already on — and works outward from there.

02

Ship the whole thing

Code, ledger, pitch deck, brand, the contract underneath it. I'd rather understand the full stack of a venture than hand off the parts that look like paperwork.

03

Build for here, first

Ghana-specific regulation, Ghana-specific margins, Ghana-specific customers. Things that work here tend to travel. Things designed for elsewhere rarely arrive.
